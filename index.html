Our Mission

Every Dog Deserves to Feel at Home

Ten years of caring for dogs of every age and temperament has taught me that trust is earned one visit at a time, not promised in a listing.

Born and raised in Seattle, I've spent the last ten years caring for the pets of friends, family, and neighbors, long before this was a business. What started as helping people I already knew kept growing by word of mouth until I decided to make it official.

I'm pet first aid certified and background-checked, with hands-on experience across dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, and more, from high-energy puppies to senior dogs who need a slower pace, and I know how to read an anxious or reactive animal and keep everyone safe. My approach is structured and calm: friendly, but focused, so every walk or stay stays organized and predictable for your pet.

I don't have pets of my own right now, so I get my fix by taking care of yours. (The cat in my photo is one of five I earned the trust of, slowly, over several months, in a single client's home. That kind of patience is what I bring to every new relationship.)

Trust, Built In Person

Every new client starts with a free meet and greet, not a form. You should feel good about who's caring for your dog before you ever leave the house.

Comfort in What's Familiar

Dogs do better in their own space, on their own schedule. My job is to fit into your dog's routine, not the other way around.

Consistency You Can Rely On

Ten years of hands-on experience means fewer surprises for you and a calmer, steadier experience for your dog.

In an Emergency

My approach is prevention-first: staying alert to hazards and avoiding unnecessary risk during every walk or stay. If a medical emergency does happen, I'll take your pet to your designated vet, or the nearest emergency vet if I can't reach you, and contact you immediately.

If I Ever Need to Cancel

If I ever need to cancel or reschedule, I'll reach out as early as possible. Same-day cancellations on my end come at no charge to you, and you'll get priority for rebooking.
