  • Weekend walks require 48 hours notice

In an Emergency

My approach is prevention-first: staying alert to hazards and avoiding unnecessary risk during every walk or stay. If a medical emergency does happen, I'll take your pet to your designated vet, or the nearest emergency vet if I can't reach you, and contact you immediately.

If I Ever Need to Cancel

If I ever need to cancel or reschedule, I'll reach out as early as possible. Same-day cancellations on my end come at no charge to you, and you'll get priority for rebooking.
